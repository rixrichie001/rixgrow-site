I'm Lakshya, founder of RixGrow. I started this because I kept seeing the same thing. Brilliant founders with real knowledge, getting outposted by people with half their value and twice their consistency.

Then I saw the second problem. Even the founders who hired clipping agencies hit a wall. The moment they stopped filming, the posts stopped too. Their best stories, the broke years and the first big win, never made it on camera at all.

So we built an agency with two engines instead of one.

We clip what you say. Then we use AI to create what your camera never could. Since then we've generated over 1 billion views for brands like Dropship.io, AutoDS, Build Your Store and Baddie in Biz.

If you've got something worth saying, we'll make sure it gets heard. Even on the weeks you say nothing.
